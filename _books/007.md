非常标准的3A线性动作冒险游戏。电影化叙事、流畅的转场和足够的大场面支撑起了整部游戏。美中不足的是关卡设置有些单一，好在将游戏时长控制在15小时左右，在玩家变得厌烦之前，及时结束了游戏。

---

A very standard AAA linear action-adventure game. Its cinematic storytelling, smooth transitions, and sufficiently spectacular set pieces are what carry the entire experience.

Its main weakness is that the level design can feel somewhat repetitive, but fortunately the game keeps its length to around 15 hours and wraps things up before the player has time to grow tired of it.
