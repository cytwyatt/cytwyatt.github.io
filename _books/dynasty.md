这是一款对我来说高开低走的游戏。前五个小时极其爽快，尤其是冲锋，放无双割草，看到屏幕上跳动的千人斩，多巴胺就会疯狂分泌。但这个游戏的缺点也极其明显，玩法爽却单调，支线无聊，度过了前几个小时的蜜月期后，快感就会极速下降。

优点：
- 建模还不错，都是帅哥美女
- 非常尊重《三国演义》历史
- 冲锋以及大军之中放无双很爽

缺点：
- 战斗玩多了略显单调
- 支线非常无聊没有任何意义
- 吕布的战力极其高，超过了所有武将之和
- 主角的魅魔人设我不喜欢
- 主线剧情谜语人，而且很无聊

---

This is a game that started strong for me but fell off hard later on. The first five hours were extremely satisfying, especially the charges, unleashing Musou attacks, and mowing down waves of enemies. Seeing the kill count on screen climb into the thousands was a real dopamine rush. But the game’s flaws are also very obvious: the combat is fun, but repetitive, and the side content is boring. Once the honeymoon phase of the first few hours is over, the excitement drops off very quickly.

Pros:

- The character models are pretty good; everyone looks attractive.
- It shows a lot of respect for the historical setting and the Romance of the Three Kingdoms source material.
- Charging into battle and using Musou attacks in the middle of massive armies feels incredibly satisfying.

Cons:

- The combat starts to feel repetitive after a while.
- The side missions are extremely boring and feel completely pointless.
- Lü Bu is absurdly overpowered, stronger than all the other officers combined.
- I do not like the protagonist’s overly “charming heartthrob” characterization.
- The main story feels cryptic and pretentious, and it is also pretty boring.
